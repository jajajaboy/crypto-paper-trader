How the strategy works

  • Every hour the bot looks at each coin's average price over the last 20 hours (fast) and the last 50 hours (slow).
  • If the fast average is above the slow one, the trend is up, so it holds the coin. If it's below, it sells and holds cash. It never borrows or bets on prices falling.
  • The $100 is split evenly across the coins, and each coin is traded independently.
  • Each simulated trade pays a 0.6% fee plus 0.05% slippage, roughly what a small Coinbase account would pay.
  • Prices come from Coinbase's free public market data. Times are shown in America/Chicago.
  • Honest note: in the 90-day backtest this strategy lost money and did worse than simply buying and holding, mostly because of fees from frequent trading. This site exists to practice and watch, not to promise profits.
